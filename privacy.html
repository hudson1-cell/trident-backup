Privacy policy

Last updated October 11, 2026.

Trident backup is a private tool used only by its owner. It copies the owner's own data, encrypted, to the owner's own Google Drive.

What it accesses

It asks Google only for access to files it creates itself (the Google Drive drive.file permission). It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Drive.

What it collects and shares

Nothing. It collects no personal information, uses no analytics or ads, and shares no data with anyone. Backups are encrypted before they leave the owner's server, and only the owner has the key.

Google user data

Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
